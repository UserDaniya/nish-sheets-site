Privacy policy — nish-sheets

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

nish-sheets is a personal tool run on the owner's own computer. It accesses Google Sheets and Google Docs only through the owner's own Google account, and only to read and update the owner's working spreadsheets and documents.

  • No data is collected from anyone else.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is used only by the owner on his own computer and is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
